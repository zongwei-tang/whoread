WhoRead

WhoRead records which programs open your sensitive files (SSH keys, tokens, browser cookies, password managers, chat history…), and which app or AI agent started them.

  • It never reads what is in your files, and it never connects to the network.
  • The history stays on this Mac, in a folder only root can read.
  • This version records and alerts; it does not block.

It installs a background service (runs as root), the menu bar app and the whoread command.
