One more step: Full Disk Access

macOS lets the background service see file accesses only with Full Disk Access. System Settings is open at Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access (if it is not, open it there):

  1. If whoreadd is in the list already and its switch is on, you are done.
  2. Otherwise click +, press Command-Shift-G, paste with Command-V (the path /usr/local/libexec/whoreadd is copied), press Return, click Open.
  3. Make sure the switch next to whoreadd is on. Monitoring starts within a minute.

Programs that were open before (Terminal, editors, AI agents) show as "started by an unknown process" until you reopen them; in Terminal a new tab is enough.

To remove WhoRead: sudo /usr/local/libexec/whoread-uninstall (add --purge to delete the history too).
